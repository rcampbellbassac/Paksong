FONTLOG for Paksong

Paksong (ປາກຊ່ອງ) is a display font for Lao, Thai, Khmer and Latin drawn from a hand-painted
sign photographed in Laos in 2019. Soft hand-painted humanist lettering: gently flared ends, elastic curves, compact Lao bowls, slight forward motion. The photo is blurred, so outlines are smoothed more.

How it was made
- Lao key letters (ງ ິ ຮ ມ ສ ເ ຕ) were traced from the photograph (scripts/segment.py, scripts/trace.py).
- The other letters were generated on the Noto designs with a simulated brush whose size and the font's width were
  measured from the traced letters (scripts/auto.py), then merged with the traced letters (scripts/hybrid.py).
- Generated letters are drafts to be redrawn toward the traced ones before 1.0.
